STUDENT TO DEAN WORKFLOW
=========================

Purpose
-------
This document explains how students, Student Assistants, Music Deans, and
Administrators work together in the current system.

The short version is:

    Student requests -> Staff Assistant submits -> System validates
    -> Music Dean reviews -> Request is approved or rejected

For quick on-site borrowing, the process is different:

    Student logs in -> Student scans RFID -> Student selects resource
    -> System records active use -> Student scans again to return

ROLES IN THE COLLABORATION
--------------------------

Student
-------
The student is the person who wants to reserve a facility or use a resource.
The student provides the information needed for a scheduled request or uses an
RFID card for self-service resources.

The student can:
- Request Cecilia Hall or MTL assistance from staff.
- Use RFID to book available cubicles, instruments, and tools.
- View only their own active RFID resource bookings after scanning.
- Return or time out their active RFID resource bookings.

The student cannot currently:
- Submit the normal Cecilia Hall booking form.
- Submit the normal MTL booking form.
- Approve or reject requests.
- Manage other users, students, inventory, or cubicle configuration.

Student Assistant
-----------------
The Student Assistant is operational staff who helps students use the scheduled
booking process.

The Student Assistant can:
- Collect the student's booking details.
- Create Cecilia Hall booking requests.
- Create MTL booking requests.
- Book or operate resource workflows allowed to staff.
- Submit a request for the student to be reviewed.

The Student Assistant cannot:
- Approve or reject Cecilia Hall or MTL requests.
- Manage users.
- Manage inventory configuration.
- Manage cubicle configuration.
- Use the approval power reserved for the Music Dean.

Music Dean
----------
The Music Dean is the reviewer and decision-maker for the applicable scheduled
booking requests.

The Music Dean can:
- Review pending Cecilia Hall requests.
- Approve or reject Cecilia Hall requests.
- Review and manage MTL booking requests according to the current permissions.
- Create bookings when needed.
- Manage department schedules and resources.
- Review rejection reasons and booking details.

Administrator
------------
The Administrator maintains the system and its data.

The Administrator can:
- Manage users and reset passwords.
- Manage student information.
- Manage inventory and equipment.
- Manage cubicle information.
- Create Cecilia Hall and MTL bookings.
- View resources, reports, and activity information.

The Administrator's role is mainly system and data management. The current
Cecilia Hall controller specifically assigns approval and rejection to the
Music Dean.

WORKFLOW A: STUDENT REQUESTS CECILIA HALL
-----------------------------------------

1. Student decides to request Cecilia Hall.

   The student prepares:
   - Name.
   - Email.
   - Preferred date.
   - Start time and end time.
   - Purpose.
   - Booker type, if required.
   - Supporting letter for an external booking, if required.

2. Student gives the details to a Student Assistant, Music Dean, or another
   permitted staff member.

3. Staff opens the Cecilia Hall booking page.

4. Staff enters the student and booking details.

5. The system validates the required fields.

6. The system checks whether the selected time conflicts with an existing
   booking.

7. The request is saved with a pending status.

8. The Music Dean reviews the pending request.

9. The Music Dean chooses one result:

   Approved:
   - The request becomes a confirmed booking.
   - It appears on the Cecilia Hall calendar.
   - The student can use the hall at the approved date and time.

   Rejected:
   - The request is not confirmed.
   - A rejection reason can explain the decision.
   - The student or staff member can be informed outside or through the
     application's available notification process.

Flow summary:

    Student
       |
       | Provides details
       v
    Student Assistant or permitted staff
       |
       | Enters and submits request
       v
    System validation and conflict check
       |
       | Saves as pending
       v
    Music Dean
       |
       +----------------------+
       |                      |
       v                      v
    Approves              Rejects with reason
       |                      |
       v                      v
    Confirmed booking      Not confirmed

WHY CECILIA HALL USES THIS PROCESS
----------------------------------
Cecilia Hall is a scheduled facility. The department needs to coordinate time,
prevent conflicts, know who is responsible for the booking, and keep a record
of the decision. Staff therefore submit the request and the Music Dean reviews
it.

The student does not use RFID for Cecilia Hall in the current system because
RFID is implemented for quick resource self-service, not scheduled hall
reservations.

WORKFLOW B: STUDENT REQUESTS MTL
-------------------------------

1. Student decides to request an MTL session.

2. Student provides the required details to staff:
   - Student name.
   - Email.
   - Student ID.
   - Date.
   - Start time and end time.
   - Purpose, if applicable.

3. Staff opens MTL Booking.

4. Staff selects or verifies the student.

5. The system checks that the student exists.

6. The system checks the student's remaining MTL time.

7. The system checks approved booking conflicts.

8. The request is submitted and waits for the applicable review process.

9. The authorized staff member reviews the request.

10. The request is approved or rejected.

11. An approved request becomes a confirmed MTL booking.

Flow summary:

    Student
       |
       | Provides student and schedule details
       v
    Student Assistant, Music Dean, or Administrator
       |
       | Enters request
       v
    System checks student, time remaining, and conflicts
       |
       v
    Pending request and review
       |
       +------------------+
       |                  |
       v                  v
    Approved           Rejected
       |                  |
       v                  v
    Confirmed MTL      Not confirmed
    reservation

WHY MTL USES THIS PROCESS
-------------------------
MTL time is linked to the student's record. The system needs the student ID to
check remaining time and make sure the booking is valid. That makes the staff
booking form more suitable for scheduled MTL reservations than the current
RFID resource workflow.

WORKFLOW C: STUDENT USES RFID RESOURCES
---------------------------------------
This workflow is for cubicles, instruments, audio-visual equipment, and tools.
It is not the current workflow for Cecilia Hall or MTL scheduled reservations.

1. Student logs in first at the browser or self-service station.

2. Student opens a normal system page. The RFID scanner component is loaded in
the application layout.

3. Student scans the RFID card.

4. The system receives the UID and searches for a matching student record.

5. The system returns the scanned student's active resource bookings, if any.

6. If the student needs a resource, the student chooses an available cubicle,
   instrument, or tool.

7. The system validates availability and booking rules.

8. The resource booking becomes active.

9. The student uses the resource.

10. The student scans again later to see their active resource bookings and
    select a booking to return or time out.

The student does not need to scan continuously. Scanning is an action trigger,
not a connection that must remain active.

The RFID response is limited to the scanned student's own active resource
bookings. It does not show other students' bookings and does not provide a
personal view of Cecilia Hall or MTL schedules.

RFID FLOW WITHOUT HARDWARE
--------------------------
A USB RFID reader normally behaves like a keyboard. It types the UID and sends
Enter. For testing without a reader:

1. Log in as Student Panel.
2. Click an empty area so no input field is focused.
3. Type a valid UID, for example:

       00203595

4. Press Enter.
5. Continue through the booking or timeout choices.

The RFID endpoint requires an authenticated session for browser testing. A
physical scanner deployment may instead use a Sanctum API token.

WHO DOES WHAT: SIMPLE EXAMPLE
-----------------------------
Example: a student wants Cecilia Hall from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM.

Student:
- Requests the time and explains the purpose.

Student Assistant:
- Enters the student's information and submits the request.

System:
- Validates the details.
- Checks for conflicts.
- Saves the request as pending.

Music Dean:
- Reviews the request.
- Approves or rejects it.

System:
- Shows the approved booking on the calendar, or keeps the rejected request
  out of confirmed bookings.

Example: a student wants to borrow an instrument.

Student:
- Logs in and scans RFID.

System:
- Identifies the student and shows the permitted resource choices.

Student:
- Selects the instrument and confirms.

System:
- Records the active booking against that student.

Student:
- Scans again when finished and selects the active booking to time out.

WHY THE STUDENT AND STAFF FLOWS ARE SEPARATE
--------------------------------------------
The two flows solve different problems.

Staff web flow:
- Best for planned reservations.
- Captures detailed booking information.
- Supports date/time scheduling.
- Supports conflict checking.
- Supports approval and rejection.
- Provides accountability for facility use.

Student RFID flow:
- Best for quick on-site resource use.
- Identifies the student at the point of use.
- Reduces manual data entry.
- Shows only the student's own active resource bookings.
- Supports quick borrowing and returning.

This separation is intended to make the system easier for students while
keeping scheduled facilities and administrative decisions under staff control.

DEMO ORDER FOR EXPLAINING THE COLLABORATION
-------------------------------------------
1. Start with a student request.
2. Show the Student Assistant entering the request.
3. Show the pending status.
4. Switch to Music Dean and approve or reject the request.
5. Show the resulting calendar status.
6. Explain that an Administrator maintains the data and system structure.
7. Switch to Student Panel.
8. Log in and simulate an RFID scan.
9. Book a cubicle, instrument, or tool.
10. Scan again and demonstrate timeout.
11. End by explaining that students do not see other students' active RFID
    bookings or staff-only scheduled booking information.

FINAL EXPLANATION
-----------------
Say:

"The student, Student Assistant, Music Dean, and Administrator work together,
but they do not perform the same action. The student provides the need. The
Student Assistant can prepare and submit a request. The system validates and
records it. The Music Dean makes the approval decision. The Administrator
maintains the system and its data. For quick resource use, the student can work
independently through RFID without waiting for a staff member, but only for the
resource types supported by that workflow."
